# M4 Rifle Master Plan

**Date:** 2026-04-06
**Status:** Initialized

## 🎯 Objective
To develop a comprehensive, high-fidelity digital and physical representation of an M4-style rifle, including exploded views, component manifests, and detailed engineering documentation.

## 📂 Directory Structure
- `docs/plans/`: High-level strategic plans and milestones.
- `docs/manifests/`: Detailed lists of parts and components for each assembly.
- `docs/visuals/exploded_views/`: High-resolution renders and technical diagrams of exploded assemblies.
